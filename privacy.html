Privacy policy

Margin Voice for Android, iPhone, iPad and Mac · 29 October 2026

What Margin Voice collects

Nothing. Margin Voice has no account, no analytics, no advertising and no crash reporting. The developer receives no data from the app.

Your voice and your documents

Voice commands and comment transcription run on your device (Android: Vosk and the on-device speech recognizer; Apple devices: Apple's on-device speech recognition). Recordings, transcripts, books and papers stay in the app's storage on your device.

Sync (optional, off by default)

If you turn on sync, Margin Voice copies your books, comments and recordings to storage you choose: your own Nextcloud or WebDAV server, or (on Apple devices) your iCloud Drive. The data goes directly from your device to that storage, under that provider's terms; the developer has no access to it. Turn sync off and delete the Margin Voice folder there to remove it.

Downloads

Sample papers you choose under Try a Sample Paper are downloaded from GitHub (github.com/kkai/marginvoice). Like any download, the request reveals your IP address to GitHub, and nothing else.

Optional voice models are downloaded from their publishers (for example Hugging Face) the first time you choose such a voice. Those requests contain no personal data beyond what any download reveals (your IP address).
